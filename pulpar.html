🌺 What Makes This Pandal Special?

Pulpar Badi Durga Maharani is a well-known Durga Puja destination in Bihar Sharif. Devotees visit to offer prayers and seek the blessings of Maa Durga.


🏰 Special attraction — Fort-inspired pandal: The pandal has featured grand historical architecture in past years. According to a report published in 2018, its design that year was inspired by Chittorgarh Fort. The design featured multiple levels, window-like openings and decorative craftsmanship.


Such themed decorations are a memorable part of the city's festive atmosphere. The exact theme and design for 2026 should be confirmed locally, as the decoration may change every year.
