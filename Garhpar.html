About Gharpar Durga Sthan

A place of faith, devotion and celebration.

Gharpar Durga Sthan is the subject of this devotional website dedicated to Maa Durga and the spirit of Durga Puja in the Bihar Sharif area.

Durga Puja celebrates the victory of good over evil. Devotees worship Maa Durga, offer prayers and seek blessings for happiness, peace and prosperity.

Puja Highlights

Feel the beauty and devotion of Durga Puja.

Maa Durga Darshan

Maa Durga Darshan – A Divine Experience 🌺🙏 The darshan of Maa Durga fills our hearts with peace, happiness, and positive energy. Her divine presence brings hope, strength, and courage into our lives. Her beautiful face and loving blessings make every devotee feel connected to the divine. 🌸✨ Maa Durga is the symbol of power, love, and protection. Visiting her pandal and seeking her blessings is a truly spiritual experience. Her divine light removes darkness from our lives and guides us towards the path of truth and goodness. Jai Mata Di! ❤️🔱 May Maa Durga bless everyone with happiness, good health, peace, and prosperity. 🌺🙏

Divine Aarti

Enjoy devotional songs and the spiritual atmosphere. 🌺 Divine Aarti – The Sacred Prayer of Maa Durga 🔱 The divine aarti of Maa Durga fills the entire atmosphere with devotion, peace, and positive energy. The sound of bells, the beautiful glow of diyas, and the soulful devotional songs create a magical spiritual experience. 🙏✨ As devotees sing the aarti with love and faith, their hearts fill with happiness and their minds become calm. The sacred light of the aarti symbolizes the victory of light over darkness and good over evil. Every prayer brings us closer to Maa Durga and reminds us of her endless love, strength, and blessings. May the divine aarti of Maa Durga bring peace to every heart, happiness to every home, and blessings to every soul. 🌸❤️ Jai Mata Di! 🙏🔱🌺

Lights & Decorations

Discover the beautiful festive atmosphere of Durga Puja. ✨ Beautiful Light Decoration – A Divine Glow The beautiful light decorations around Maa Durga's pandal create a magical and heavenly atmosphere. 🌟✨ Colorful lights, glowing diyas, sparkling stars, and elegant decorations illuminate the entire place with divine beauty. The golden lights make Maa Durga's idol look even more beautiful and majestic. The combination of vibrant colors and glowing lights fills the surroundings with joy, devotion, and festive energy. 🌺🙏 As the lights shine brightly, the entire pandal feels like a heavenly place where faith meets beauty and devotion meets happiness.
