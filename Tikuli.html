About Tikulipar Durga Puja

A celebration of devotion, faith and happiness.

Tikulupar Durga Puja is a pandal destination searched for by devotees celebrating the festival in the Bihar Sharif area of Bihar.

During Durga Puja, devotees worship Maa Durga and celebrate the victory of good over evil. Families visit pandals, offer prayers and enjoy the festive atmosphere.

Note: The exact history, annual theme, programme timings and current-year pandal photographs should be confirmed locally before adding specific details.
